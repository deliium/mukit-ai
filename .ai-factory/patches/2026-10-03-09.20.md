**Severity:** medium
**Tags:** execution-node, concurrency, cancel, verification-gap

## Problem

`/aif-verify` found Task 12 incomplete: worker busy gate used a hardcoded `_ACTIVE >= 64` and never advertised `busy`/`draining` from `resources.max_concurrency`. Task 10 acceptance covered pre-cancel and `task_not_found` but not controller→worker cancel forward, and generate `_invoke_chat` was unwired in tests.

## Root Cause

v1 ship prioritized register/heartbeat/fake complete_text. Concurrency was stubbed at the schema max (64) instead of the worker's advertised `max_concurrency`, and heartbeat always sent `available`. Cancel acceptance stopped at operation-trace pre-check after earlier DNS/ASGI flakes with the slow fake path.

## Solution

- Add `AI_EXECUTION_NODE_MAX_CONCURRENCY` (default 2, clamp 1–64) on settings.
- Gate `dispatch_complete_text` on `_DRAINING` or `active >= max_concurrency` → `execution_node_busy`.
- Expose `worker_resource_snapshot()` for heartbeat/register; `busy` at capacity, `draining` when the worker loop stops.
- Acceptance: slow fake + controller `cancel_task` forward; `_invoke_chat` seam test with ChatOpenAI boom.

## Prevention

When a plan task says "bound from resources.X", assert the runtime gate and the heartbeat field against the same setting in one test. For cancel, require a slow path that keeps the controller task index populated until cancel forwards.
